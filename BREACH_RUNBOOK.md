# Breach Runbook (DPDP Act Compliance)

## 72-Hour Notice Requirements
Under the DPDP Act, any personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Board of India and the affected Data Principals (users) as soon as possible, and generally within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

## Steps
1. Identify and contain the breach.
2. Assess the risk to the rights and freedoms of users.
3. Notify the Data Protection Board.
4. Notify affected users.
