Privacy Notice

This privacy notice is a developer draft. All sections marked ***LEGAL REVIEW*** must be finalized by counsel before publishing.

1. What personal data we collect

  • Inputs you provide in the app (planner tasks, exam dates, GPA entries, attendance data). These are persisted in your browser `localStorage` by default.
  • Server-side data for uploaded PDFs and AI prediction runs (metadata such as department, subject code, paper year). These are stored in our Supabase database.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser user-agent, and timestamps captured when you submit consent or rights requests.

2. Why we process your data (purposes)

  • Core service: storing and presenting your planner, attendance, and GPA so the app functions.
  • AI predictions: using subject and paper metadata to produce study suggestions.
  • Analytics & product improvement: only where you opt in.
